BOB MARLEY

The King of Reggae & Pan-African Icon

One of the pioneers of reggae, Marley's music became a global cry for peace, justice, and revolution. He remains the most influential figure in Jamaican history.

The Life

  • Born: Feb 6, 1945
  • Origin: Nine Mile, Jamaica
  • Died: May 11, 1981 (Miami, FL)
  • Known As: Tuff Gong
  • Genres: Reggae, Ska, Rocksteady

Achievements

  • 1978: UN Peace Medal of the Third World
  • 1994: Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Inductee
  • 1999: "Exodus" named Album of the Century
  • 2001: Grammy Lifetime Achievement Award

"When you smoke herb, herb reveal yourself to you. All the wickedness you do, the herb reveal itself to yourself, your conscience... because herb make you meditate."
