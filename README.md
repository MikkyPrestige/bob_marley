# Bob Marley: The Legend Tribute Page

A modern responsive tribute page dedicated to the King of Reggae, **Bob Marley**.

---

## 🦁 Why Bob?
Bob Marley is more than just a musician to me; he is a global symbol of resilience and peace. I built this project because his message of "One Love" and his unwavering stand for justice have been a constant source of strength in my own life.

While the world changes, his music remains a "natural mystic" that reminds us to stay grounded, keep our heads up, and live with integrity. This page is my way of keeping that fire burning and sharing his legendary journey with others.

## Features

- **Modern UI/UX**: A sophisticated dark theme with "Gold, Green, and Red" Rastafarian accents.
- **Responsive Design**: Fully fluid layout that looks great on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Interactive Discography**: A comprehensive grid of over 100 entries, including:
  - Studio & Live Albums
  - Compilations & Remixes
  - Box Sets
  - **Non-Album Singles**: Organized in a scannable multi-column grid.
- **Curated Links**: Direct access to high-quality recordings of career-defining milestone tracks.
- **Clean Typography**: Utilizing *Montserrat* for impactful headers and *Lora* for readable storytelling.

## Built With

* **HTML5**: Semantic structure for better accessibility.
* **CSS3**: Advanced CSS Grid and Flexbox for the complex discography layout.
* **Google Fonts**: Custom typography integration.
